Machine vision solutions

Automated visual inspection for zero-defect production

Manual visual checks get tired by the end of a shift. A Karmix vision system inspects every part the same way, in milliseconds, and saves an image of each result for your records.

Presence & absence

Confirms every component, clip, screw and label is in place before the part moves on.

OCR & print verification

Reads printed and laser-marked text, dates and part numbers and checks them against the expected value.

Barcode & Data Matrix

Reads 1D and 2D codes, grades their quality and links each part to its traceability record.

Dimension measurement

Non-contact gauging of lengths, diameters, gaps and positions against your drawing tolerances.

Surface defect detection

Finds scratches, dents, burrs, cracks and contamination, including with AI-based inspection for varied defects.

Colour & sequence check

Verifies wire colours, harness sequences, LED colours and the right variant of a part.

Connector & pin inspection

Checks pin count, pin position, bent or pushed-back pins and connector seating.

Robot guidance & alignment

Locates parts for pick and place, screwing and dispensing, and corrects position and angle on the fly.

How a Karmix vision station works

  1. 01Lighting & opticsThe right camera, lens and lighting chosen for your part, finish and field of view.
  2. 02Image captureTriggered by a sensor or the PLC as the part arrives, at line speed.
  3. 03Inspection softwareRule-based and AI tools measure, read and compare against your limits.
  4. 04OK / NG decisionA clear result for the operator, with the failed region marked on screen.
  5. 05Reject & recordThe PLC diverts NG parts and the image and result are saved against the serial number.
