What we build for your production line

One partner from requirement to installation. Every solution is made for your product, your station layout and your quality process, so you get a system that fits instead of one you have to work around.

Testing

Catch faults at the station, before they reach your customer.

  • ECU testingCAN and LIN based functional testing with PEAK-System hardware, diagnostics and DTC checks.
  • FCT & EOL test stationsFunctional and end-of-line testers for PCBs and finished assemblies, with limits and reports.
  • IC flashing & programmingProduction flashing with ST-LINK, Renesas and Cyclone programmers, with checksum verification.

Traceability & software

Know the history of every serial number, on demand.

  • Traceability applicationSQL and API based tracking of parts, test results, operators and stations across the line.
  • Printing & scanningBarcode, QR and label printing applications linked to your part data.
  • Custom software & integrationDesktop, database and web tools, plus API integration with your ERP, MES or customer portals.

Automation & machines

Stations and machines that run reliably, shift after shift.

  • PLC & HMI solutionsControl logic and operator screens that are clear to use on the shop floor.
  • Assembly line & indexer machinesStation automation and indexing machines built for precision and repeatability.
  • IoT & BLE devicesWireless connectivity and device communication for smart products and machines.
